Drone Zones UK · effective 16 October 2026

Drone Zones UK ("the app") is published by Cloud Phoenix Limited ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 12906101), registered office C/O Aardvark Accounting, 1 Cedar Office Park, Cobham Road, Wimborne, BH21 7SB, United Kingdom. Contact: dronezones@cloudphoenix.co.uk.

In short

  • No account, no adverts, no analytics or tracking.
  • The app doesn't collect or send us any of your personal data. It has no servers of its own. (If you leave a tip or contact us, see Tips, reviews and feedback.)
  • The app talks directly to a few public services (listed below) to show the map and data. Like any internet request, those services see your device's IP address and the request itself.

What stays on your device

  • Your location. Used only when you tap My location, to centre the map and check that spot. It isn't stored and isn't sent to us.
  • Saved places, settings and choices (map type, layers, maximum height).
  • Downloaded data: airspace restriction zones, NOTAMs, the app's settings file, and map tiles you've viewed (kept so the map works without signal). You can clear map tiles under About › Data on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Tips, reviews and feedback

  • Tips are optional and paid through Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details. Google gives us basic order information (such as an order number, the item, price, date and country), which we keep for our accounts and to handle refunds.
  • Reviews are made in Google Play's own review screen and published by Google under your Google account name. The app doesn't see what you write.
  • Feedback. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply and improve the app. Posts on our GitHub feedback page are public and handled by GitHub under its own terms.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13.

Your rights

The app doesn't collect personal data about you. If you've tipped or emailed us, you can ask us for a copy of, or to delete, what we hold (except order records we must keep for tax and accounting). For data a third-party service may hold, contact that service. If you have a concern, email us at dronezones@cloudphoenix.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
