Drone Zones UK

By Cloud Phoenix Limited

Drone Zones UK shows where you can fly your drone in the UK. It puts the official NATS UAS flight restriction zones (airport FRZs, prisons, military and security sites, restricted and danger areas) and current NOTAMs on a map, and tells you whether a spot is clear, needs care, or needs permission.

  • Restriction zones update automatically every 28-day AIRAC cycle; NOTAMs at least hourly.
  • Tap the map, search a place or postcode, or use your location to check a spot.
  • Plan ahead: check a date and time up to six days ahead, with NOTAM daily schedules taken into account.
  • Ground height for each spot, so zones measured from sea level get a definite answer.
  • Map and satellite views; areas you've viewed still show without signal.
  • Save your regular flying spots.
  • No account, no adverts, no tracking.
Before you fly. Drone Zones UK is a planning aid, not an official briefing, and isn't endorsed by NATS or the CAA. Check the UK AIP and NATS pre-flight information, follow the Drone Code, and get the landowner's permission.
