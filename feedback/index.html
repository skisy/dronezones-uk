Feedback and support

Drone Zones UK

Drone Zones UK is made by a small, independent developer. Ideas, bug reports and kind words all help shape what comes next, so thank you for taking the time.

Suggest a feature

Something you'd like the app to do? Have a look at the ideas so far (add a 👍 to the ones you'd use) or suggest a new one.

Report a problem

App not working as expected, or a zone looking wrong? Report it on GitHub. In the app, About › Copy app details copies your app version and data dates, which helps a lot.

Wrong roads or places on the base map come from OpenStreetMap, and anyone can fix them there. Restriction zones and NOTAMs are published by NATS: the app shows them as published.

Posts on GitHub are public and need a free GitHub account. Please don't include personal details, such as your home location. If you'd rather not post publicly, email dronezones@cloudphoenix.co.uk instead.

Support the app

The app is free, with no ads and no tracking. If it's useful to you, here's how you can help:

  • Rate and review it on Google Play. It's the biggest help in getting the app in front of other pilots.
  • Tell your flying club or other drone pilots about it.
  • Leave a tip in the app: About › Support Drone Zones UK. It's paid through Google Play and doesn't unlock anything. The app is the same for everyone.
